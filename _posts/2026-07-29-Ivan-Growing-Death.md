I might make this into a series of juxtaposing scenarios that I see existing in the world, but for now I'll write about the "Growing Death".

For the last one hundred years, we have evolved as a society, as a collective human race. We sacrificed millions of lives so that we may live in peace, so that tomorrow is a guarantee, and not an idea. 

Yet as we move through the years, that vision seems to grow dimmer, almost overcast by the ideas that yet again, tomorrow becomes an idea. I say this with caution. I live an arguably privileged life, where I can sit and write about my existence. Nonetheless, it feels that my tomorrow sits in the hands of men I don't know. Our world is carved up in meeting rooms, politicians, and billionaires deciding what will happen, how and when. They write the scripts, and present it to us like breaking news. 

This all comes during a time when we are mean to be "free", when folk before us have protested, fought and won rights for others. But look around. We are free but some voices are silenced, and others can shout. We are free, but some can tour the world, while others don't see further than the local shopping centre, for that's all they can afford. We are free, we can vote, and yet, the change and progress we want is merely further and further away. 

The "Growing Death" is my view that while we don't fight wars for our survival (us UK folk), we fight for it through the ever growing artificial intelligence, political instability, and other shit that doesn't make sense. Most of us are defined by our accolades, what we achieve and how young we get it. And this is our death. We don't have space to creak, to learn and make mistakes. We are so pressured by our environments to succeed, to make a statement in this desperate world. That's only for those who can get into that sphere. There's an epidemic of highly qualified specialists working in bars, pubs, coffee shops and elsewhere. And those who dare step of the beaten path? Uncertainty, doubt, and fear, towering over us daily, for what, the desire to follow our dreams and ambitions?

As I say, we live in a time where we should be able to do what we wish, how we wish. And yet, that seems more and more closed off. Maybe I'm wrong, but the way I see it, is that our free water is taken by data centres, politicians and the rich can do as they wish, murder children, bomb countries, while I have to pay council tax or I'm a criminal, or that I can't safely go off and do as I wish. I can't travel, become a cook, read for days, learn film and photography, or even write. Original ideas are rare, and I implore you to ask and challenge me on mine. As we grow as a collective, to be "better", we get less freedom, and there's arguably more and more death. This the "Growing Death".
