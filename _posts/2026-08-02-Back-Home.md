Hi friends and strangers,

I recently went back home and I think this is the first time I’ve ever felt home sick.

While I’ve been in this country for 4 years and counting, something is different.

Compared to my uni days, I no longer have my friends, I’m living in a city full of strangers, no one that looks like me, behave like me or someone who shares my experiences.

For the first time ever I’m learning what missing home feels like.

Everyone always says they miss the food back home but to me its much more than that.

Home is where I get to hear my siblings laughter while we’re in the car trying to survive my sisters terrible driving.

Where I get to hear my mom WhatsApp calling my extended family echo throughout the house, be subjected to the typical questions, “how long are you back for”, “when are you coming back again” and  “when are you going to get married”.

Home is where my father nags about my finances and I have to reassure him that I’m doing alright by presenting him my monthly money excel sheet for the 500th time.

Home is where I get to hear my local dialect from every corner and get discounts if I strike small chats with the shop owner.

Home is where I get to talk to my friends non-stop for 6 hours and getting to switch seamlessly between English and Malay, never having to find what the right word is in a foreign language.

Home is where I get to rediscover the cultural mixing pot that Malaysia is, the hospitality of our people and how it’s truly like no other.

But also back home is … 

Where I can’t escape terrible commuting, wasting 3 hours of my day in endless traffic.

Back home, I would be at the mercy of egos of my senior colleague, not being accepted for my outspoken nature and how I’m white washed so I wouldn’t understand how seniority works.

Back home is where I have to choose doing something I enjoy or something that pays me enough.

Back home is where I will be consumed by work from 9 am to 9 pm, 6 days a week and barely be alive when Sunday comes along. 

Back home is where I would feel like I’m in a constant competition with my peers and I’m just this tiny, lost creature in a rat race.

I replay this internal battle every day, weighing the pros and cons, the what ifs and the foolishness of it all , how I could possibly complain about having a good paying job abroad.

Till I come to a decision, I’ll keep listening to my 2000s Indo-Malay band songs, reminiscing over my childhood at my grandmother’s house, listening to these songs on the radio and life was a lot simpler.
